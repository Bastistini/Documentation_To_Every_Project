## Player Fantasy
Collect and Avoid - collect objects to win (5-8). Avoid the Enemies.
scared, stressed, what kind of player(character)?



## Pillars
Three short rules that protect the fantasy. A good pillar says no to something.

1. No Weapons - you cant defend yourself. just survive
2. when an enemy is near, they will chase you, unless you are looking at them. Have to look. when not looking, the enemy chases you. And can catch you. 
3. No hiding, must move around all the time, no safe space.


## Core Loop
What the player does again and again, as 3 or 4 verbs.

> Find the objects, avoid the enemy, look at the enemy to freeze them, find all objects to Escape

## Win and/or Lose
How does it end? You need at least one.

- Win: find all to win
- Lose: getting caught, not finding the objects in time

## Risk
Something that could stop your game working, or make it late. Then: how will you test it first?

> Having the enemy follow you, and then disappears for a moment when you flash, to then start to chase you again. Only allowing the enemy to chase you when are not looking.
making a Weeping angel like Ai, chasing you. when flashed, teleport away and then reappear somewhere else.

## Opportunity
Something that could make your game special.

> Have a FlashLight that scare away the enemy to give you some calming moments.
